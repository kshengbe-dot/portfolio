Analytics Case Study

E-commerce Sales Analysis & Revenue Insights

An end-to-end SQL and Tableau project focused on customer purchasing behavior, revenue distribution, and product performance in an e-commerce environment.

Multi-Table Relational database design
SQL Advanced queries & analysis
Tableau Dashboard & storytelling
Business Focus Insight-driven decisions

Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce order data to uncover patterns in customer behavior, product performance, and revenue generation. The goal was to simulate a real-world analytics workflow by transforming structured datasets into actionable business insights.

Business Context

E-commerce businesses rely heavily on understanding customer spending patterns and product performance. Identifying high-value customers and top-performing products enables better marketing strategies, pricing decisions, and inventory management.

Objective

  • Identify top customers by total spending
  • Analyze revenue trends over time
  • Evaluate product and category performance
  • Support data-driven business decisions

Database & Structure

  • Designed relational database with customers, products, orders, and order_items
  • Established relationships using primary and foreign keys
  • Structured data for efficient querying and analysis

Analysis & Methodology

  • Used SQL joins to combine multiple tables
  • Applied aggregation functions (SUM, COUNT, AVG)
  • Identified revenue drivers and high-value customers
  • Analyzed trends using time-based queries

Visualization & Reporting

  • Exported SQL results into CSV for Tableau
  • Built dashboards showing revenue trends and customer insights
  • Used data storytelling to highlight business performance

Key Insights

  • A small group of customers generated the majority of revenue
  • High-value products contributed significantly more than low-cost items
  • Revenue trends revealed clear growth opportunities over time

Business Impact & Recommendations

  • Focus marketing efforts on high-value customers
  • Prioritize premium product categories
  • Use revenue trends for forecasting and planning
